A box whose first piece (first line, row or child) does not fit in what is left of the page now
starts on the next page whole. Its top margin, border and padding used to stay alone at the bottom
of the page while its content moved on, so the content started higher on the new page than the
same content on a page of its own. Two-line paragraphs at the bottom of a page now follow
`orphans`/`widows` this way too.
